# Enterprise product objective

Build a sellable, production AI-assisted Tableau-to-Streamlit-in-Snowflake accelerator for varied, previously unfamiliar workbooks. Preserve the original accelerator's working end-to-end capabilities, correct generic original defects and V3 regressions in V3, and prove correctness independently.

The customer journey is connect/upload, assess, verify sources, convert, compare, review exceptions, approve deployment and operate the application. Deliver the app, provenance, acceptance evidence, exceptions and operational handover. Use [the canonical enterprise handover](V3_ENTERPRISE_HANDOVER.md) for the complete understanding.

## Required product outcomes

- Correct source identity, data ingestion, relationships, calculations and filter order.
- Supported charts, tables, labels, totals, parameters, interactions, layouts and accessibility.
- Cortex assistance in assessment, mapping, calculation/semantic/visual proposals, diagnosis and migration guidance, with measured quality/cost.
- Independent numerical, visual, interaction, security and complete-workbook acceptance.
- Secure multiuser operation, customer-configurable installation, complete packages, monitoring, recovery, upgrades and rollback.
- A published supported scope and measured customer value before commercial claims.

## Preservation and safety

The original already includes AI utilities and broad rendering infrastructure. Reuse qualified components. Keep exact field/source/pane/occurrence identity; forbid workbook/sheet/filename/caption-specific dispatch. Never silently omit required filters, data or calculations. Cortex proposals cannot establish their own acceptance, invent reference data or authorize executable model SQL/Python.

## Release meaning

Internal acceptance requires one complete workbook followed by all five selected cases. Controlled pilots require unfamiliar customer workbooks within declared scope. Enterprise release requires repeatable independent acceptance plus installation, security, concurrency, operations and commercial evidence. All gates remain subject to [the roadmap](V3_PRODUCT_ROADMAP.md) and [tracker](V3_ENTERPRISE_FIX_TRACKER.md).

A deployment, successful SQL execution, correct synthetic formula, accepted AI plan, rendered chart or passing documentation audit is not enterprise product completion. The current product remains an engineering candidate. No arbitrary-workbook coverage, ROI percentage, universal pixel parity or committed timeline is established.
