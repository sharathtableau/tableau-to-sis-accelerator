Why We're Doing This
02 / 10

A new metric, and two teams waiting on each other

A retail client, real engagement. The ELT team adds a new metric to the Snowflake tables — their cycle completes, the data is live. But nobody in the business can see it yet. It still needs a Tableau developer to build it into the dashboard and push it through Tableau's own deployment cycle. A second cycle, stacked on the first, on its own clock and its own queue.

Leadership didn't ask us to make the Tableau cycle faster. They asked why there was a second cycle at all.

🔀
Two cycles, one deliverable

ELT ships the metric to the table. The BI layer then needs its own build-and-deploy pass before anyone sees it.

⏳
Data ready ≠ data visible

A metric sits fully loaded in the warehouse while it waits in the BI queue. The lag is organizational, not technical.

🎯
The ask was structural

Not a faster intermediate layer — no intermediate layer. If it's in the table, it should be in the dashboard.
